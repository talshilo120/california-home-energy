LADWP rebates Los Angeles homeowners should know about in 2026

Most of Los Angeles is served by the LADWP (Los Angeles Department of Water and Power), which runs some of the largest residential efficiency rebate programs in the state:

  • Up to $2,500 per ton for a qualifying heat pump HVAC system
  • Up to $2,500 for a heat pump water heater
  • Rebates for smart thermostats, attic insulation, cool roofing, and efficient windows

Good to know: LADWP rebate amounts, tiers, and funding change throughout the year, and some rebates require specific equipment efficiency ratings or a participating contractor. Your contractor confirms current eligibility and exact amounts for your address at the time of your assessment.
