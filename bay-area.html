Bay Area rebate programs in 2026

The Bay Area has several overlapping programs — which one applies depends on your exact city:

  • San Jose Clean Energy (SJCE): up to $2,500–$3,500 for a heat pump water heater, plus a $500 attic-insulation add-on
  • BayREN: regional electrification and efficiency incentives across the nine Bay Area counties
  • Silicon Valley Clean Energy (SVCE): water-heater and electrification rebates for Santa Clara County cities outside San Jose

Which one applies to you? SJCE covers San Jose; SVCE covers many other Santa Clara County cities; BayREN spans the wider region. Knowing your territory changes your rebate — that's exactly what an assessment sorts out. Full San Jose & Bay Area rebate guide →
