San Diego rebate programs in 2026

San Diego's incentives look a little different from the rest of the state — the biggest opportunity is often in your yard:

  • $2 per square foot for replacing lawn with approved water-wise landscaping (up to 5,000 sq ft) through the SoCal Water$mart program — a potential $2,000 on a typical front lawn
  • $100 per tree planted (up to 5) as part of a turf-replacement project
  • SDG&E product rebates — smart thermostats, efficient water heaters, and more

Read before anyone sells you turf "with a rebate": the water-rebate program explicitly excludes synthetic/artificial turf. Artificial turf is a great low-maintenance choice on its own — but the $2/sq ft rebate applies to qualifying water-wise plants and permeable surfaces, not fake grass. Full San Diego rebate guide →

Common questions

Is the San Diego assessment really free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation, and you're never charged for the assessment itself.

Does artificial turf qualify for the water rebate?

No — the SoCal Water$mart program excludes synthetic turf. The rebate applies to qualifying water-wise plants and permeable surfaces. Your contractor can design a yard that captures the rebate where it applies.
