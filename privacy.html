Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

California Home Energy Center ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices and rights you have — including rights specific to California residents.

1. Information We Collect

When you complete a form on this site, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your first and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address and ZIP code
  • Homeownership status, approximate home age, and the improvements you're interested in
  • Your preferred time to be contacted

We may also automatically collect limited technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) through standard web analytics and cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Contact you to schedule and confirm your free home energy assessment
  • Connect you with a licensed contractor in your area
  • Determine which programs or upgrades your home may be eligible for
  • Respond to your questions and improve our services

3. Consent to Be Contacted

By submitting a form, you agree that we, and the licensed contractor(s) we work with, may contact you by phone call, text message (SMS), and email at the number and address you provided — including through an automatic telephone dialing system or prerecorded/artificial voice — regarding your free home assessment. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you may ask to be removed from our calling list at any time.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share your information with the licensed contractor(s) and service partners who perform your assessment and contact you. We may also use trusted service providers (for example, form and email delivery services) to operate this website. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with unrelated third parties for their own marketing without your consent.

5. Cookies & Tracking

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for basic functionality, analytics, and advertising measurement. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information you submit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how we use and share it
  • Request access to or a copy of your personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (note: we do not sell your information)
  • Not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

8. Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults (18+) who own residential property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
