SMUD rebates Sacramento homeowners should know about in 2026

Sacramento sits in SMUD territory — and here's the good news: while the federal energy tax credits expired at the end of 2025, SMUD actually raised several rebates for 2026:

  • Up to $3,000 for a qualifying gas-to-electric heat pump HVAC conversion
  • Up to $4,000 for a gas-to-electric heat pump water heater
  • Additional rebates for smart thermostats and other efficiency upgrades

Good to know: SMUD rebates require installation by a contractor in the SMUD Contractor Network, and amounts are subject to program terms and funding availability. Your contractor confirms current eligibility. Read the full Sacramento SMUD rebate guide →
